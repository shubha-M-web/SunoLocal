Platform Mission & Integrity

Why SunoLocal Matters to Mysore & Karnataka

Empowering students, farmers, job seekers, and citizens with verified local information

The Everyday Information Barrier
  • Information Fragmentation: Agriculture subsidies, sports trials, scholarship updates, and jobs are scattered across 25+ government portals (SSP, Seva Sindhu, KEA, KSCA, Raita Mitra).
  • Language & Bureaucratic Jargon: Gazette notices are dense PDF documents written in administrative jargon that citizens and students struggle to navigate.
  • Unverified Circulars & Fake Forwards: Unverified WhatsApp messages mislead citizens with fake application deadlines and unauthorized fees.
The SunoLocal Solution
  • Unified All-Category Discovery: Search any question across agriculture, sports, education, tech, jobs, health, or weather with intelligent intent understanding.
  • Voice Accessibility in English & ಕನ್ನಡ: Complete text-to-speech audio briefings with Play, Pause, Resume, and Stop controls on any device.
  • TrustCheck Integrity Audit: Strict source verification, official government domain checks, and clear separation between verified official notices and demonstration records.
